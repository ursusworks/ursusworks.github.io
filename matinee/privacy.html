Privacy policy

Applies to Matinee for Xbox. The same text is in the app under Settings → About → Licenses → Privacy.

Matinee talks only to the servers you add: your Emby server, and Sonarr and Radarr if you connect them. It has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts of its own, and sends nothing to the developer.

Stored on this console, in Matinee's own app storage: the servers you added and their sign-in tokens, Sonarr and Radarr addresses and API keys, your settings, and a random device id that your Emby server uses to recognise this console. Uninstalling Matinee removes them; signing out removes that server's token.

Your Emby server receives what any Emby app sends: what you play, playback progress and the device name. That data stays on your server, under your control.

Some artwork that Sonarr and Radarr point to (cast photos, episode stills) is loaded directly from the image services they use, such as image.tmdb.org and artworks.thetvdb.com. Those requests reveal your IP address to those services, as a web browser would.
