Roadmap

What's planned for Matinee. This is a guide, not a promise: there are no dates, and plans can change.

Planned next

Control Matinee from your phone. Pick Matinee as a "Play on" target in the Emby app, and start or control playback from there.

Music. Album and artist pages, a Now Playing screen, and music that keeps playing when you leave the app. Background playback has strict memory limits on Xbox, so this depends on testing on the console first.

Live TV guide. A programme guide, and changing channel from inside the player.

Later

Collections and playlists. Proper pages for both, with Play all.

Easier sign-in and users. Sign in with Emby Connect or a PIN, and switch user without signing out.

Pages for shows that aren't in Emby yet. A full page for a show that's only in Sonarr, like upcoming movies from Radarr today.

Styled subtitles. ASS/SSA subtitles with their original fonts and positioning.

Trailers on Home. Muted trailers in the spotlight.

More languages. Matinee is English only today.

Recently added

1.2.5. Trailers on movie and show pages, and resuming starts a few seconds back so you pick up the thread.

1.2. Theme songs, frame-rate matching, smoother scrolling, and DTS:X and Dolby Atmos confirmed through to an AV receiver.

1.0. Preview pictures while seeking, faster and lighter browsing, and a support site.

0.8. A–Z jump and more filters in libraries.

0.7. A Licenses page, and a couple of seasonal surprises.

0.6. Sonarr's missing and upcoming episodes on series pages, and movie pages from Radarr.

0.5. Intro and credits on the timeline, and an Up next countdown.

Missing something? Suggest a feature on the issue tracker.
